Until We Tie the Knot!


Step into the timeless elegance

of a Jeffersonian-inspired manor set against a stunning backdrop just outside Washington, DC. With its stately architecture, sparkling crystal chandeliers, and beautifully designed ballrooms, the estate creates an unforgettable setting for weddings, refined gatherings, and special occasions.

Sunlit interiors, grand French doors

and intricately carved fireplaces lend a romantic charm to every celebration, while both indoor and outdoor ceremony spaces offer flexibility for your perfect day. Spanning 22 private acres, the estate features sweeping views, spacious event areas, ample on-site parking, and a distinguished façade that brings a sense of classic grandeur to any event.

Nestled in the scenic hills

of Northern Virginia’s wine country, Rose Hill Manor's rolling landscapes and picturesque views, paired with versatile indoor and outdoor spaces, allows hosts to bring their unique vision to life. Whether planning an intimate gathering or a grand celebration, Rose Hill Manor continues to be a sought-after location where meaningful moments and lasting memories are made.
