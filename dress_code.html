Until We Tie the Knot!


We kindly invite our guests to join us in formal attire as we celebrate in the Virginia countryside this fall. Think elegant and elevated - floor length gowns or sophisticated cocktail dresses for women, and suits or tuxedos for men.

~

With the crisp autumn air and setting in mind, rich seasonal colors, textured fabrics, and layered pieces are warmly encouraged.

~

Please also consider appropriate footwear for grass and outdoor pathways to stay comfortable while enjoying the beautiful countryside surroundings.
