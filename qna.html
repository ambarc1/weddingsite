Until We Tie the Knot!


Rose Hill Manor

All events including the ceremony and reception will be located at Rose Hill Manor 42140 Raspberry Plain Lane Leesburg, VA 20176. More information about Rose Hill Manor can be found here.

Venue Parking is Available

The venue has 105 parking spaces available. We recommend using a rideshare service and/or carpooling. Ride-sharing options should be inexpensive and readily available.

Food Will Be Available

We will provide food at the reception cocktail hour and have dinner at reception. However feel free to grab a light bite or drink during the gap. A snack never hurt anyone!

Plan to Fly Into Washington-Dulles (IAD)

Dulles is the best option and should have flights to just about anywhere at a reasonable price. We recommend booking in advance while rates are still low.

There Are Plenty of Lodging Options

Leesburg has plenty of hotels and Airbnbs in the local area. We recommend booking the Hotel Burg, Hampton Inn & Suites Leesburg, Holiday Inn Express & Suites Leesburg, Homewood Suites by Hilton Leesburg, or any Airbnb options in the scenic Leesburg area.
